## ⚠️ PROJECT DEPRECATED

The XANESNET project (2020–2023) is **no longer maintained or hosted here** and **active development has stopped**.  

I recommend switching over to the fork maintained by the Penfold Group and the Research Software Engineering (RSE) team @ Newcastle University - the XANESNET project is still under active development there and continues to receive regular updates and new features:  

👉 [XANESNET (Penfold Group + RSE Team Fork)](https://github.com/NewcastleRSE/xray-spectroscopy-ml)  

Thank you to all the friends and colleagues who have contributed to and supported the XANESNET project over the years!
